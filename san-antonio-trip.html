15 Verify before going

Discrepancies — not silently resolved

Zoo on Thanksgiving (Nov 26): one aggregator says closed; the zoo's own $8 Community Locals Days on Nov 26–27 and the Thanksgiving-week guide say open. Verify on sazoo.org. The $8 locals rate is likely Bexar County residents only.

LEGOLAND hours: venue listing Mon–Sat 10–6, Sun 11–6; Tiqets says 10–5 daily with last entry 3 PM; a reseller says last entry 4:30 PM.

LEGOLAND Thanksgiving: one listing calls it open Nov 26, but LEGOLAND's own FAQ warns of holiday closures. Unverified.

Kiddie Park hours: official site (Mon–Thu closed, Fri–Sun 9–5) vs. other sources listing 10–5/10–7 and Monday hours. Official site wins; still verify.

SEA LIFE Sunday hours: 11–5 vs. 11–6 across sources.

Six Flags Nov 27–29 hours: noon–9 PM (park calendar) vs. 2–9 PM (one guide).

Botanical Garden November hours: official site says 9 AM–4 PM daily; some resellers list 8 AM–6/7 PM (stale summer hours) — use 9–4.

Witte adult admission: $15 vs. $17 — channel-dependent.

Zoo Lights start: official zoo sources say Nov 14; one weekend guide said "Opens Nov 21" — official wins (Nov 14–Jan 3).

Hexed runtime: 1h 43m vs. 1h 49m across sources.

Magik Grinch ticket price: $26.50–$33.50 vs. $30 adult / $25 child 2–17 + fees.

Natural Bridge Caverns hours: official listing Mon–Fri 10–4, Sat 9–5, Sun 9–4 vs. resellers listing 8:30–5:30 daily.
